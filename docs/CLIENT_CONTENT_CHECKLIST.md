# Client content and asset checklist

Use this checklist before publishing the site. The website should distinguish a proposed model from a facility or programme that is already operating.

## Organisation details
- Confirm the exact registered name and preferred public spelling: “FPC Bazar Federation” / “FPC Bazaar Federation” appears inconsistently in source documents.
- Confirm the official legal entity type and the exact wording supported by the registration certificate and MCA records.
- Confirm the registered office address, phone number, email and website. The supplied profile and earlier project notes contain differing contact details.
- Confirm which registration documents may be displayed publicly.

## Leadership and institutional engagements
- Confirm each person's current name, designation and spelling.
- Confirm whether the person described as Chairperson is in fact the Chairperson. The supplied profile explicitly identifies Naveen Dhingra as MD; do not interchange roles.
- For event photographs, confirm date, venue, event title, people pictured and permission to publish.
- Do not imply government endorsement, official affiliation or a formal partnership from a photograph alone.

## Gaushala model
- The tourism and integrated Gaushala material is a proposal/concept. Confirm the site, operating status, facilities, implementation partners and services before describing any item as operational.
- The 80–120 employment figure in the presentation is an estimate/projection, not verified current employment.
- Confirm any claims about income doubling, self-sustainability, visitor services, eco-stay and product availability.

## Other initiatives and claims
- Confirm written permission before using Amul name, logo or brand assets.
- Verify all Enzybiotic product, partner and efficacy claims.
- Verify government scheme names, formal support, institutional affiliations and FPO network claims.
- Do not publish “zero investment”, assured buyback, loan percentages or guaranteed returns without approved written terms.
- Treat FPO and youth employment numbers on the homepage as vision targets, not achieved results.

## Documents and privacy
- CIN may be shown as a public organisation identifier after checking the certificate.
- Do not publish the full PAN card/PAN number.
- Publish MOA, licences and other legal documents only after the client approves a public/redacted copy.
- Never expose private contact details or identity documents of individuals.
